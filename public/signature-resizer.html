Note the direction of the limit. Most exams set a maximum, so smaller is safe. UPSSSC and EPFO set a minimum too, so a signature squeezed down to 10 KB is rejected for being too small. Always check whether your form says up to or between.

Why signatures get rejected

Compressing too far leaves the strokes broken or grey, and many boards reject a signature written entirely in capital letters. A signature photographed at an angle, on lined or coloured paper, or with a shadow across it also fails. Sign in black ink on plain white paper, then resize.

How to resize a signature in KB

  1. Sign on white paper in black ink, then photograph or scan it straight on.
  2. Open PhotoResizer and upload the signature image.
  3. Enter the KB your form asks for - 10, 20, 30 or 50 KB - and the pixel size if given.
  4. Crop the whitespace so the signature fills the frame, then download the JPEG.

What is the normal signature size in KB for exams?
Most Indian government exams ask for 10 KB to 20 KB in JPEG at roughly 140 x 60 pixels. UPSSSC wants 30 to 50 KB and EPFO 20 to 50 KB, so check your notification.

How do I reduce my signature from 100 KB to 20 KB?
Upload it to PhotoResizer and set the target to 20 KB. It reduces the file without letting the strokes break up, which is what happens when you keep re-saving in a phone gallery app.

My signature is too small in KB - how do I increase it?
Rescan or rephotograph it at a higher resolution rather than upscaling a small file, then set your target inside the required range. Forms like UPSSSC reject signatures below their minimum.

What pixel size should a signature be?
About 140 x 60 pixels is the standard for most exam portals, matching a 3.5 x 1.5 cm scan. Some forms state centimetres instead of pixels.
