Frequently asked questions

What is the photo size for IBPS?
IBPS forms typically need a JPEG photo of about 20–50 KB, around 200×230 pixels. Confirm on the current notification.

What is the signature size for SBI Clerk?
Usually a JPEG signature of 10–20 KB on white paper with dark ink. Check the exact SBI notice.

Do bank forms need a thumb impression?
Often yes — IBPS and SBI may require a left-thumb impression (about 20–50 KB) and a handwritten declaration (about 50–100 KB).
