What is the photo size limit for TCS NQT?
TCS iON portals generally cap the photograph at 100 KB in JPEG. Aiming for about 90 KB leaves headroom so the upload is not rejected at the boundary.

Generally up to 20 KB in JPEG. Around 15 KB is a safe target that still keeps every stroke visible.
