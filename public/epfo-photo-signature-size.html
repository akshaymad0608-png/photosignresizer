Why EPFO uploads get rejected

A signature written in CAPITAL LETTERS is explicitly not accepted by EPFO. The other common causes are uploading the photo in the signature slot (or the reverse), which EPFO warns will get your admission to the exam denied, and a file outside the KB range.

Frequently asked questions

What is the photo size for the EPFO SSA form?
A recent colour passport-style photograph in JPEG, about 20 KB to 50 KB, at roughly 200 x 230 pixels, scanned at 3.5 x 4.5 cm.

What is the EPFO signature size?
A JPEG at roughly 140 x 60 pixels signed in black ink on white paper. Recent EPFO notifications specify 20 KB to 50 KB, while older ones used 10 KB to 20 KB, so check your notification.

Can I sign in capital letters for EPFO?
No. EPFO states that a signature in capital letters will not be accepted. Use your normal running handwritten signature in black ink.

What size photo does the EPFO UAN portal need?
The UAN member portal profile photo allows a larger file than the recruitment form, generally up to about 100 KB. Set that target in PhotoResizer and download.
