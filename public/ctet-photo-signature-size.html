CTET Photo & Signature Size

The photo and signature limits to aim for when you fill the CTET or a state TET form, and a quick way to hit them.

Preset values, confirm on your notification · By PhotoResizer · Free · No sign-up · Works on mobile

Quick answer

PhotoResizer's CTET / TET preset uses a photo of 10–100 KB at 350 × 450 px and a signature of 3–30 KB at 350 × 150 px, both JPEG. Always compare with the official notification before you upload.

Teacher eligibility tests are filled in by lakhs of candidates, often from a phone and often close to the last date. That is the worst moment to find out that your signature is 400 KB or that the photo looks stretched.

The signature range is unusually low

The signature range in this preset starts at only 3 KB, which is much lower than most exam forms. That is because a clean signature on white paper compresses very small. If your signature file lands in the low single digits, it is usually fine as long as the strokes are still readable when you zoom in.

Practical tips

  • Photograph the signature on plain white paper with a black or blue pen and crop tight around it, so the pixel budget is not wasted on empty paper.
  • For the photo, keep your head centred and your face unobstructed. Do not use a filter or a heavily edited picture.
  • If you also apply to KVS or NVS, note that PhotoResizer has a separate preset for them with a different signature range. Choose the preset that matches the form in front of you.
