Thumb Impression Size & Resizer

Bank and insurance forms ask for a left thumb impression as a separate upload. Here is the size it has to be, and how to hit it.

Last updated: August 2026 · By PhotoResizer · Free · No sign-up · Works on mobile

Quick answer

Most bank forms want a left thumb impression as a JPEG of 20-50 KB at about 240 x 240 px, in black or blue ink on plain white paper. It is a square upload, unlike your signature. Set the size in PhotoResizer and download an upload-ready file.

The thumb impression is square, roughly 1:1, while your signature is a wide rectangle - so you cannot reuse the same crop for both. If the notification asks for a right thumb impression instead, follow the notification. IBPS PO, Clerk and RRB forms, SBI recruitment and NICL forms all use this upload.

Why thumb impressions get rejected

A smudged impression where the ridges have run together is the most common rejection, usually from too much ink or from moving while pressing down. Press once, firmly, and lift straight up. After that it is the crop: a photo of the whole sheet of paper leaves the thumb tiny inside a wide frame, so crop tight to a square around the impression before you resize to 20-50 KB.

How to resize your thumb impression

  1. Take the impression with your left thumb in black or blue ink on plain white paper.
  2. Photograph or scan it straight on, in good light, with no shadow.

Frequently asked questions

What is the thumb impression size for bank exam forms?
A JPEG between 20 KB and 50 KB at roughly 240 x 240 pixels, about 3 x 3 cm, taken with the left thumb in black or blue ink on white paper.

Left thumb or right thumb?
Bank forms generally specify the left thumb for all candidates. If your notification says otherwise, follow the notification.

Which exams need a thumb impression upload?
IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk and IBPS RRB forms, SBI recruitment forms and NICL forms all ask for a thumb impression alongside the photo, signature and handwritten declaration.

Why does my thumb impression look smudged?
Too much ink, or moving your thumb while pressing. Use a thin, even coat, press once firmly and lift straight up without rolling. The ridge lines need to stay separate.

Can I crop my thumb impression from a scan of the whole page?
Yes. Crop tight to a square around the impression so it fills the frame, then set the target KB. A thumb lost inside a wide frame reads as a blank upload.
