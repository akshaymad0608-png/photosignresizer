Frequently asked questions

What is the standard passport size photo dimension?
In India it is 3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm). Some visas and forms use 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) — always check the specific requirement.

How do I make a passport size photo online for free?
Upload your photo to PhotoResizer, set the passport size and required KB, then download — free, no watermark, in your browser.

What background should a passport photo have?
A plain white or light background with an evenly lit, clear face and no shadows.

Can I print 4 or 8 passport photos on one sheet?
Yes — make the 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo, then print multiple copies on a 4 × 6 inch sheet at a photo lab or printer.
