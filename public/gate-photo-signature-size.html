Why GATE photo uploads get rejected

Unlike most exams that only check a KB range, GATE also enforces an aspect ratio — a photo cropped to the wrong width-to-height proportion gets rejected even if the file size and pixel count look fine. The signature has the same issue in reverse: a very short, wide scan often fails the 1:2.75–1:3.75 ratio check. Crop to the ratio first, then compress to the KB range — doing it the other way round is what causes most repeat rejections.

Frequently asked questions

What is the photo size for GATE?
GATE needs a recent colour passport-size photo (3.5 x 4.5 cm) in JPEG, between 5 KB and 600 KB, sized 200x260 to 530x690 pixels, with the face covering 60-70% of the frame. Confirm the exact limits on the current GOAPS notification.

What is the signature size for GATE?
GATE signatures are 3 KB to 300 KB in JPEG, sized 250x80 to 580x180 pixels, handwritten in black or dark blue ink on white paper.
