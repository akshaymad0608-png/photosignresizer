The India Post GDS application asks for a passport-style photograph and a scanned signature. Most rejections are not about the picture itself. They happen because a phone photo is several MB, or because a signature scan is too large or too faint.

Why a phone photo gets rejected

A modern phone camera produces a file of 2 to 6 MB at thousands of pixels wide. The GDS form expects a small file, so the upload field either refuses it or the page times out. Shrinking the pixel dimensions first and then fixing the KB size gives a much cleaner result than squeezing the original with heavy compression.

Practical tips

  • Use a plain, light background and face the camera straight on. Avoid caps, sunglasses and strong shadows across the face.
  • Sign with a dark pen on plain white paper and photograph it in daylight. A faint grey signature turns into a smudge once it is compressed to 10–20 KB.
  • Keep the file as JPEG. Renaming a PNG to .jpg does not convert it and the portal may still refuse it.
