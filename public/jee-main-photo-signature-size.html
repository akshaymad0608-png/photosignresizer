JEE Main Photo & Signature Size

How to prepare the photo and signature for the JEE Main application, with the sizes our preset is built around.

Quick answer

PhotoResizer's JEE Main & Advanced preset uses a photo of 10–200 KB at 350 × 450 px and a signature of 4–30 KB at 350 × 150 px, both JPEG. Always compare with the official notification before you upload.

Students filling the JEE Main form are often 17 or 18 and doing it for the first time, usually with a parent helping on a phone. The photo and signature step is where many people get stuck.

This page explains the sizes the JEE Main & Advanced preset uses and how to produce files that fit them. It does not replace the exam agency's instructions.

Photo first, signature second

The photo range is wide, so you have room to keep a sharp image. The signature range is narrow and small, and that is where compression matters. Resize the signature on its own, and keep the pen stroke dark on white paper so it survives the reduction.

How to resize your photo and signature

  1. Keep your originals — Use the unedited photo and the scan or picture of your signature, not copies that have already been compressed.
  2. Open PhotoResizer — Choose the JEE Main & Advanced preset from the exam list, or enter custom values if your notification differs.
  3. Upload and check the preview — Add the photo, then the signature, one at a time. Look at the preview before you accept it.
  4. Download and check the size — Save the JPEG files and confirm the KB size in your file manager before uploading to the form.

Practical tips

  • Use a recent photograph with a plain background. A scan of an old printed photo usually looks uneven and may be refused.
  • Sign inside a clear box drawn on white paper, then crop to the signature. A very wide empty margin shrinks the actual writing.
  • Do not edit the signature with filters. Straightening and cropping are enough.

Frequently asked questions

What is the JEE Main photo size?
The preset uses 350 × 450 px with a file between 10 KB and 200 KB in JPEG. Check the current information bulletin for the session you are applying to.

What is the JEE Main signature size?
The preset uses 350 × 150 px and 4 KB to 30 KB in JPEG. Verify against the bulletin.

Does this also work for JEE Advanced?
The preset covers both names, but the registration portals are separate. Read the JEE Advanced instructions for the exact requirement.

Can I resize on my phone?
