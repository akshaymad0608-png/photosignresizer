SSC Photo & Signature Size (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD)

SSC now captures your photograph live on its application form. The only image you upload is your signature: a JPEG of 10-20 KB.

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · By PhotoResizer · Free · No sign-up · Works on mobile

Quick answer

Photo: captured live by the SSC form - nothing to upload. Signature: JPEG, 10-20 KB, about 6.0 x 2.0 cm for SSC GD and about 4.0 x 2.0 cm for CGL. Resize it with PhotoResizer with the SSC GD Constable preset (or the SSC preset for CGL).

What to do about the photo

Older guides tell you to resize an SSC photo to 20-50 KB at 3.5 x 4.5 cm. That no longer applies: the form takes the picture itself. Stand or sit in good light with a plain background, keep the camera at eye level, look straight ahead, keep your whole face inside the guide area, and remove any cap, mask or glasses. Do not photograph a printed photo - SSC rejects it.

Common reasons SSC uploads get rejected

A blurred or miniature signature, a signature cropped to the wrong shape (SSC GD's box is about three times wider than tall, so a signature cropped for another exam gets squashed), a file outside 10-20 KB, and a live photo taken of an existing picture. Sitting other exams too? See the IBPS, RRB and UPSC size guides.

How to resize your SSC signature

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a dark pen, large enough to fill the width.
  2. Scan or photograph it flat in good light so it is sharp.
  3. Open PhotoResizer and pick the preset for your exam: SSC GD Constable for the 3:1 signature, or SSC for the 2:1 CGL shape.
  4. Set the file size to 10-20 KB and download the JPEG.

Frequently asked questions

What is the photo size for SSC CGL and GD?
There is no photo file to resize. The SSC application module captures a real-time photograph through your webcam or phone camera while you fill in the form, and a capture of an old or printed photograph is rejected. What matters is good light, a plain background, looking straight at the camera, your whole face inside the guide area, and no cap, mask or glasses.

What is the SSC signature size?
A scanned signature in JPEG format between 10 KB and 20 KB. The image size depends on the exam: the SSC GD notice says about 6.0 x 2.0 cm, and CGL is reported at about 4.0 x 2.0 cm. Blurred or miniature signatures are rejected.

How do I resize my signature for SSC GD?
Choose the SSC GD Constable preset in PhotoResizer. It crops to a 3:1 shape (about 6 x 2 cm, 360 x 120 pixels) and keeps the file at 10-20 KB. The full steps are on our SSC GD Constable guide.

Can I upload my old photo to an SSC form?
No. SSC states that photographs are captured live and that capturing a pre-existing photograph leads to rejection. Candidates who register with Aadhaar-based authentication are not rejected on photo or signature quality grounds.
