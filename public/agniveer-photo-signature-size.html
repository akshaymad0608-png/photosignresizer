Agniveer Photo Resizer — Photo & Signature Size

The three Agniveer portals do not use the same rules. Air Force in particular wants a sky blue background and a slate with your name on it.

Quick answer

Agniveer photographs are generally 10-50 KB at about 350 x 450 px in JPEG. The catch is the background: Army and Navy want a plain light or white background, while Air Force (Agniveer Vayu) requires a sky blue background and a black slate held with your name and photo date in white chalk capitals. Why Agniveer uploads get rejected

For Air Force it is almost always the background and the slate: a white-background photo is rejected where sky blue is required, and the black slate must show your name and the date the photo was taken in white chalk, in capital letters. Across all three services, signing in block capitals is prohibited - the signature must be in running hand. The rest is file size, since a phone photo is several megabytes against a 50 KB cap.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Agniveer photo size?
Generally a JPEG between 10 KB and 50 KB at about 350 x 450 pixels for Army, Navy and Air Force Agniveer applications.

Why does Air Force Agniveer need a blue background?
The Agniveer Vayu portal specifies a sky blue background, unlike Army and Navy which want a plain light or white background. A white-background photo submitted to the Air Force portal is rejected.

What is the black slate in the Agniveer Vayu photo?
Air Force applicants hold a black slate showing their name and the date the photograph was taken, written in white chalk in capital letters. It has to be legible in the uploaded image.

What is the Agniveer signature size?
Usually 10 KB to 50 KB in JPEG, though Army entries have specified as little as 5-10 KB and some Air Force cycles as much as 80-150 KB. Check the limit shown on the portal you are applying through.

Can I sign in capital letters for Agniveer?
No. All three Agniveer portals prohibit signatures in block or capital letters. Sign in your normal running handwriting with a black or dark blue pen on plain unruled white paper.
