BPSC Photo & Signature Size

The BPSC preset has a very narrow KB window. Here is how to land inside it without trial and error.

Preset values, confirm on your notification · By PhotoResizer · Free · No sign-up · Works on mobile

Quick answer

PhotoResizer's BPSC (Bihar) preset uses a photo of 20–25 KB at 350 × 450 px and a signature of 10–15 KB at 350 × 150 px, both JPEG. Always compare with the official notification before you upload.

A 5 KB window is easy to miss by hand. Compress a photo a little too much and it drops under 20 KB, too little and it sits above 25 KB. Doing this in a generic editor can take many attempts.

Why the narrow window is awkward

JPEG file size does not change smoothly with quality, and it depends on how much detail the picture has. A busy background makes the file bigger at the same quality. Using a plain background gives the tool far more room to hit a small target.

How to resize your photo and signature

Practical tips

  • Take the photo against a plain wall, so there is less detail to store.
  • Crop the signature tightly. Less white paper means more of the small budget goes to the actual strokes.
  • Re-check the final size in your file manager before uploading. The number shown there is what the portal will see.
